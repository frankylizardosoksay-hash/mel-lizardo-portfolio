Nothing good was ever designed alone.

I design learning with clients, partners and the people who will actually use it. Teacher first, anthropologist by training, learning designer by trade.

Every culture has worked out how to pass on what it knows. Apprenticeships, stories, rituals, classrooms, and now whatever we are building with AI.

I am interested in the part that does not change: someone has something worth knowing, someone else needs it, and between them sits a design problem. I spent years solving it in a social studies classroom. I now solve it for universities, government partners, organisations and skills providers.
